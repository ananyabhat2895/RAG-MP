SEPASAL medicinal-use enrichment audit
=======================================

Defect found: yes.
The medicinal CSV schema has USubsetText but no Use34Text. The original processor therefore copied Plant Parts correctly into use_category but did not obtain detail values from sepasal_uses_specific.csv. Its bibliography-specific detail join also missed valid detail rows when bibliography records differed.

Use_category is a direct mapping of USubsetText, so Plant Parts is not a mistaken mapping. The medicinal CSV has no Use34Text, Plant Parts value, or detailed-use column. The old empty arrays were a processor join defect, not evidence that Moringa lacked detail: sepasal_uses_specific.csv provides roots, humans, oedemas, and poultices for TaxKey 1308 / the same Use12Text.
